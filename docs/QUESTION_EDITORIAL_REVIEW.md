# Question Editorial Review

The starter question bank is draft educational content. Every new item begins
with `reviewStatus: "pending-editorial-review"`. Inclusion in the repository does
not mean that a question has received religious or historical approval.

Before changing an item to `approved`, an editor should:

1. Check the fact and its intended scope against reliable Sirah references.
2. Record the references actually consulted in the editorial workflow. Never
   invent a citation or add one from memory.
3. Confirm that exactly one option is unambiguously correct and that the other
   choices are plausible without being misleading.
4. Review the Bahasa Melayu and English meaning, spelling, respectful wording,
   transliteration, and age-appropriate reading level.
5. Confirm that the explanation teaches the fact without presenting a disputed
   report as settled history.
6. Ask a suitably qualified content reviewer to examine sensitive or disputed
   material. Revise or remove anything that cannot be stated safely.

Keep question IDs and option IDs stable after release because saved answers and
analytics may refer to them in the future. Meaningful answer changes require a
fresh editorial review. `sourceNotes` are internal reminders only; they must not
be presented to players as citations or evidence.
